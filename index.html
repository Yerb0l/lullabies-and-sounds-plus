Privacy Policy

Lullabies & Sounds Plus  ·  Last updated: April 29, 2026  ·  Developer: 22RedStudio  ·  Contact: admin@22redstudio.com

Who this policy is for

Lullabies & Sounds Plus (the "app") is a lullaby and sleep-sound playback app intended to be played for infants, toddlers and young children in their bedtime environment. The app is therefore designed with the assumption that the listener may be a child under the age of 13, and is operated by their parent or legal guardian.

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what data the app collects, what it does not collect, and how we comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including its provisions for children (GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy.

Summary

  • The app does not require an account, sign-in, name, e-mail address, age, gender, location, or any other personal information.
  • The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, behavioural data, or identifiers with us, with Google, or with any third party.
  • The app does not show advertising of any kind.
  • The app does not include analytics, tracking, crash-reporting SDKs, or social-media SDKs.
  • The app does not access the internet for any purpose other than the standard Google Play services framework that ships with every Android app.
  • All settings, favorites and audio recordings stay on the user's device. They are never sent anywhere.

Data we collect

The app stores the following information locally on the device only, in the app's private storage area, and the data never leaves the device:

What Where Why
Theme preference (light/dark) Android DataStore To remember the user's display choice
List of favourite tracks Android DataStore To populate the Favorites screen
Last selected sleep-timer length (minutes) Android DataStore To preselect the same value next time
Audio files recorded by the user via the microphone App's private files directory So the user can play back their own voice or song as a lullaby
Audio files imported by the user from device storage App's private files directory So the user can play their own audio files inside the app

This data is removed automatically when the app is uninstalled, when the user uses the in-app delete actions, or via Android's "Clear data" option in System Settings.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • RECORD_AUDIO — only requested when the user taps the in-app microphone button to record their own lullaby. The recording is saved as a file in the app's private storage and is never sent anywhere.
  • ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY — used solely to allow the parent/guardian to toggle the device's "Do Not Disturb" mode from inside the app, as a convenience while putting a child to sleep. The app does not read or modify any other notification setting.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — required by Android so that audio playback continues while the screen is off and the app is in the background.

The app does not request: location, contacts, calendar, SMS, phone, camera, body sensors, network state for tracking, advertising ID, accounts, biometrics, or any other sensitive permission.

Microphone recordings

When the user taps record, the microphone is active only for the duration of that recording session. The captured audio is written to a file inside the app's private files directory (/data/data/com.redstudio22.lullabies/files/recordings/) and is accessible only to this app on this device. The file is never uploaded, shared, or analysed by us. The user can delete each recording from inside the app at any time, which permanently removes the underlying file from the device.

What we do NOT collect

To be unambiguous: we do not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, photos, contact lists, browsing history, IP addresses, device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, IMEI, MAC, serial numbers), location (precise or approximate), voice content, usage metrics, crash reports, or anything else.

Third parties

The app does not integrate with any third-party advertising, analytics, marketing, social, or attribution SDK. The app does not contain in-app purchases. The app does not embed third-party content players.

The only third-party services the app relies on are:

  • The Android operating system — provides the platform on which the app runs.
  • Google Play services framework — distributes the app and delivers system updates. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not control what data Google itself collects at the operating-system level.

We have no contractual relationship with any other third party in connection with this app.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K, Google Play Families Policy)

We have designed this app with the understanding that the listener is often a child under 13 (and may, depending on local law, be considered a child up to age 16 or 18). Accordingly:

  • The app collects no personal information from any user, whether child or adult, beyond what is described above (which is stored only on the device and never transmitted).
  • The app does not profile users, build behavioural models, or serve personalised content of any kind.
  • The app does not show ads, including contextual or behavioural.
  • The app does not contain links that take users out of the app to external websites, social networks, or marketplaces — except for a single "Send feedback" button which opens the device's e-mail client pre-filled with our support address. No data is sent automatically; the parent/guardian decides what, if anything, to type and send.
  • The app does not include in-app communication features (no chat, no messaging, no voice or video calls, no comments).
  • The app does not store data in any cloud service.

Because no personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared, no parental consent mechanism is required under COPPA or GDPR-K for the app's normal use. Parents/guardians remain in full control: uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data immediately.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data stays on the device, deletion is fully under the user's control:

  • In-app: tap the heart icon on a track to remove it from Favorites; long-press a recording in My Recordings and choose Delete to permanently erase the file.
  • System: open Android Settings → Apps → Lullabies & Sounds Plus → Storage → Clear data, to wipe all app-stored data at once.
  • Uninstall: removing the app removes all of its data with it.

We have no servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export, or delete on your behalf.

Your rights

Under GDPR (including GDPR-K), CCPA and similar laws, users generally have the right to access, correct, port, restrict, or delete personal data held about them, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing to access, correct, port, or transfer on our side. Local data is fully accessible to and deletable by you on the device, as described above.

Security

Data stored by the app uses Android's standard private-storage sandbox, which prevents other apps on the device from reading it without root access. We do not transmit any data, so there is no data-in-transit to secure. We do not run servers, so there is no back-end to secure either.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data — for example, adding an optional cloud-backup feature in a future version — we will update this policy, change the "Last updated" date at the top, and prominently flag the change inside the app before the new behaviour becomes active. Material changes that affect children's data will require renewed parental consent in the manner required by the applicable law at that time.
